Organise the kickabout, collect the cash, track the games.

SubUp is for friend groups who play together — football, rugby, padel, basketball, and the rest. Private groups, a proper lobby, pay-to-join, scores, Man of the Match, and a simple rating. Not a public court marketplace.

How it works

  1. Create or join an invite-only group with your mates.
  2. Admins put a game on: time, venue, min/max players, pitch cost.
  3. Players join and pay (Apple Pay, Google Pay, or card). No pay, no spot.
  4. If too few people have paid before the lock window, the game auto-cancels and everyone is refunded.
  5. After kickoff, the admin enters the score. Players vote Man of the Match. Ratings update.

Your account

You can delete your SubUp account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete account. That removes your login, memberships, and upcoming spots. Past games may show you as “Deleted user”. If you no longer have the app, use this deletion request page.

If you are the last admin of a group that still has members, promote someone else first. Groups where you are the only member are deleted with your account.
